For a **formal grievance** concerning FairSwap accounts, listings, safety, privacy or platform conduct, use the existing [Grievance Redressal page](https://fairswap.in/grievance). That page is the current source for the designated officer, contact route and submission guidance.

## Submit a grievance through the official channel

1. Read the grievance page for the current contact information and relevant grievance category. Do not assume the Help Centre's ordinary email-support button replaces that formal route.

2. Describe the issue accurately, including relevant dates and a listing URL, user reference or conversation context if applicable. Provide only information necessary to investigate the issue.

3. Do **not** email passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, complete bank or card credentials, Aadhaar scans or unrelated identity documents. Keep copies of your submission and any acknowledgment securely.

4. For general account troubleshooting, use ordinary support instead. If the problem is an ongoing financial cyber fraud, promptly contact your bank and use the applicable official [cybercrime reporting channels](https://cybercrime.gov.in/); a platform grievance is not a police complaint.

The authoritative FairSwap grievance notice remains on **fairswap.in**. This Help Centre article does not establish a separate statutory process, deadline, remedy or compensation promise.
