Formal grievance guidance is available on FairSwap’s existing grievance page. It is distinct from routine tickets and marketplace reports.

## Practical steps

1. Read the current official grievance page for the designated contact method and required information. This draft does not replace that notice.

2. Describe the issue and relevant dates clearly. Share only information needed to handle the grievance through the official channel, not in public comments.

3. Keep your submission and any acknowledgement securely. For privacy requests read the current privacy policy. This guide promises no statutory deadline, outcome or compensation.
