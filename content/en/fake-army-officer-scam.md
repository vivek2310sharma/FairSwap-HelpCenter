A person claiming to serve in the armed forces is not proof that a marketplace deal is genuine. This concerns impersonation, not legitimate service members.

## Practical steps

1. Do not rely on a uniform photo, identity-card image or emotional story. These can be copied. Judge the transaction by inspection and independent payment checks.

2. Refuse advance deposits, supposed military transport fees or payments demanded before you can inspect the item. Pressure and repeated new fees are warning signs.

3. Stop the transaction if details do not match. Save the conversation and payment references securely for a report; do not post someone’s identity documents publicly.
