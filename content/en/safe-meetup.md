A listing, seller profile or friendly conversation does **not** guarantee that a person or an item is safe. Plan an in-person exchange that you can leave at any time.

## Before and during the meetup

1. Choose a well-lit, populated public place during daylight. Tell someone you trust where you are going, and consider bringing a friend. Do not disclose your home address unnecessarily.

2. Agree in advance on the item, price or exchange terms, time and meeting place. Be cautious if the other person repeatedly changes the arrangement or pushes you towards a secluded location.

3. Inspect the item and included accessories before handing over money or your own item. Test electronics where practical and independently check authenticity or ownership for higher-value goods. You can leave if the item differs from the description.

4. For UPI payments, verify the transaction in **your own** banking or payment app. A screenshot or a message saying "paid" is not proof of a credit. Receiving money does not require your UPI PIN or accepting an unexpected collect request.

Avoid deposits or advance courier fees merely to reserve an item. See [FairSwap's safety guidance](https://fairswap.in/safety) for additional precautions.
