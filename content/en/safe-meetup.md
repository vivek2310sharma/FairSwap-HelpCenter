Plan an exchange where you can inspect the item and leave safely. A chat agreement or a profile alone does not guarantee safety.

## Practical steps

1. Agree on a busy public place in daylight. Tell someone you trust where you are going and consider bringing them. Do not give a stranger your home address unnecessarily.

2. Inspect the item, accessories and condition before deciding. Ask to see it working when appropriate; walk away if someone changes the arrangement or pressures you.

3. Confirm payment in your own bank or payment app, not a screenshot. Never share an OTP or UPI PIN. Do not send an advance deposit merely to reserve a meeting.
