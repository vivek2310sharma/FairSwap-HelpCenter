Private ticket submission and replies stay on FairSwap, not this Help Centre. You may need to sign in to the marketplace.

## Practical steps

1. Open the official account support page. Follow the current options displayed there rather than relying on an unverified category name.

2. Explain what happened, what you expected and which platform you used. Include relevant listing context; redact private details from screenshots.

3. Check replies in the existing account support area. Do not share ticket details publicly or expect a fixed response time. If you cannot sign in, check official contact guidance.
