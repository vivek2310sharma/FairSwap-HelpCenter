FairSwap's support-ticket system runs on the **main marketplace**, not on this public Help Centre. You need to sign in to create and track account support tickets.

## Use the existing support dashboard

1. Sign in to FairSwap and open [Help & Support](https://fairswap.in/account/support). The page redirects unauthenticated visitors to the FairSwap sign-in screen.

2. Choose the available **New Inquiry** option. Complete the required category, subject and message fields according to the form shown. State what happened, what you expected and which device or platform you used. Include a listing link or relevant details when useful.

3. Submit the inquiry and check the same support area for its status and staff replies. If you have an existing ticket, open it there to continue the conversation rather than creating duplicates.

4. If you **cannot sign in or cannot create an account**, do not get stuck in the ticket login loop: use **Email support** below. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, UPI PINs or full payment credentials in a message.

Moderation appeals may also be handled through the existing account support system. Response times and ticket categories may vary; this guide promises no fixed SLA.
