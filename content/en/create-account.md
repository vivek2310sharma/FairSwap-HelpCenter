Start from the official marketplace and follow its current sign-in instructions. Screen names and app steps may differ; these instructions need product review.

## Practical steps

1. Open fairswap.in directly and check the address before entering account information. Avoid login links sent by an unknown buyer or seller.

2. Use the account entry point and follow the options shown there. Keep verification codes private; support or another trader should not need your OTP.

3. If sign-in fails, check the connection and the details you entered. Avoid repeated requests; open the existing contact guidance for help rather than sharing codes.
