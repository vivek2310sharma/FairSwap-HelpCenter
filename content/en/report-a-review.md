Ratings describe a trading experience; they are not a safety certificate. These instructions describe Ratings V1 when enabled on the Web marketplace. Availability and labels may differ by app; if controls are missing, use support.

## Steps and limits

1. For a misleading, abusive or otherwise concerning visible published review, use its report control when available and explain the specific issue in 3–500 Unicode code points. This technical text limit also applies to replies: an emoji or a written character made from several code points can use more than one. You cannot report your own review. One report per person per review is accepted, with at most ten new cases per rolling 24 hours; repeated reports do not increase priority. If controls are unavailable, use the support routes below.

2. The recipient can add one permanent reply only to a visible published review that contains written text. A reply is not available for a faces-only review. Keep it factual and within 500 Unicode code points; it cannot be edited after submission and does not change the original rating. Do not publish personal details or retaliate.

3. If you were selected as buyer for a transaction you did not complete, use the transaction dispute action before its original 14-day deadline. A dispute freezes review submissions and suppresses public reviews and counted reputation until authorized resolution. It does not automatically delete anything or refund money. After the deadline, use support or formal reporting instead.

4. Preserve the listing link, review or transaction reference if available, dates, relevant conversation screenshots and bank/UPI transaction references. Keep originals securely; share necessary evidence privately with official support or authorities. Never send OTPs, PINs, passwords, full card details or unrelated personal information, and do not post evidence publicly.

5. Authorized moderators may inspect the reported review, replies and transaction evidence, including otherwise sealed review material where required for the case. They may hide or restore a review, resolve a dispute or adjust transaction eligibility. An allegation or tag is not proof of fraud. Reporting does not automatically remove a review or account, guarantee a result or guarantee repayment.

6. For follow-up, sign in to [existing support tickets](https://fairswap.in/account/support). If you cannot sign in, email [FairSwap support](mailto:support@fairswap.in). For a formal grievance follow the [existing grievance route](https://fairswap.in/grievance). Keep the case reference private. These routes do not require a new Help Centre account.

## Stay safe

Never scan a QR code or enter a UPI PIN to receive money. Never share OTPs. Check credits in your own bank or UPI app; screenshots can be fake. Beware of fake buyer payment links, army-officer impersonation and courier scams. Do not send advance fees to establish trust, unlock payment or obtain a better rating.

For suspected financial cybercrime in India, promptly contact your bank/payment provider and report through [the official cybercrime portal](https://cybercrime.gov.in/) or call **1930**. Reporting does not guarantee recovery.
